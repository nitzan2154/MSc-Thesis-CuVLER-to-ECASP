\chapter{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Accurate 6DoF object pose estimation enables applications such as robotic manipulation, augmented reality, and surgical navigation.
Yet unlike a class label, bounding box, or segmentation mask, a 6DoF object pose cannot generally be annotated by sight.
It requires a geometric object model and accurate object-to-camera alignment, making pose supervision costly, particularly under occlusion and in specialized environments.
Synthetic rendering offers exact pose labels from CAD models, but RGB-based training introduces a synthetic-to-real gap through differences in texture, material, illumination, and background~\cite{tremblay2018deep}.
This dependence is especially problematic for textureless or reflective objects, whose appearance provides weak and unstable pose cues.

We therefore investigate calibrated multi-view silhouettes, which provide complementary geometric constraints without relying on texture or material appearance.
Given the input binary masks, pose estimation is invariant to changes in color, texture, material, and illumination that leave the silhouettes unchanged.
Binary masks can be rendered without photorealistic assets, substantially reducing the RGB synthetic-to-real gap.
The remaining challenge is geometric: silhouettes contain no internal appearance cues and may be incomplete or ambiguous under occlusion and symmetry~\cite{laurentini1994visual,Guan2010}.
Recovering pose from these incomplete silhouettes therefore requires jointly reasoning across all calibrated views.

Our formulation decouples object segmentation from pose estimation.
Since masks are easier to annotate than accurate 6DoF poses, a segmenter can be adapted independently to the target environment.
We evaluate ECASP with benchmark masks to isolate pose recovery and separately examine predicted masks from a fine-tuned segmenter.

We introduce ECASP, \emph{\textbf{E}pipolar \textbf{C}ross-\textbf{A}ttention for \textbf{S}ilhouette-Based \textbf{P}ose Estimation}, a calibrated multi-view model that estimates the 6DoF pose of known rigid objects from silhouettes under occlusion, Fig.~\ref{fig:teaser}.
ECASP uses epipolar cross-view attention to fuse silhouette evidence according to camera geometry and a hybrid geometric--learned pose head to recover 6DoF pose.
Despite being optimized exclusively on synthetic silhouettes and using no RGB appearance, ECASP is competitive with RGB-based multi-view methods on the real MVPSP surgical-object benchmark~\cite{hein2025surgicalnavigation}.

This work underscores the following contributions:

\begin{itemize}
    \item We formulate calibrated multi-view 6DoF pose estimation from silhouettes, showing that CAD-rendered masks can support accurate real-data pose estimation without photorealistic rendering or domain randomization.

    \item We develop a calibrated fusion architecture that adapts epipolar cross-view attention to multi-view silhouette features.

    \item We evaluate the practical mask-input assumption using masks from a
    fine-tuned segmenter and analyze robustness to characteristic segmentation
    errors.
\end{itemize}
